Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

RevUp is operated by NahaLabs (Pty) Ltd ("we", "us"), Johannesburg, South Africa. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and your rights under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, password (stored hashed), display name and industry choice.
  • Workspace content: posts, drafts, media, schedules, approval activity and settings you create.
  • Connected channels: access tokens and profile identifiers for social accounts or WhatsApp numbers you connect, stored encrypted.
  • Enquiries and inbox data: messages received through channels you connect, so the service can display and draft replies to them.
  • Usage data: log data needed to operate, secure and debug the service.

How we use it

  • To provide the service: drafting, scheduling, publishing, responding and analytics.
  • To communicate with you about your account (verification, password resets, service notices).
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party processors

We use infrastructure and service providers (cloud hosting, email delivery, and the third-party platforms you choose to connect) to operate RevUp. Each processes data only as needed to provide its function, and your use of connected platforms is also governed by their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account from your account settings; account deletion removes your personal information and workspace content, subject to short backup and legal-retention windows.

Your rights (POPIA)

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may object to processing. Contact us to exercise these rights. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa).

Security

We use encryption in transit, hashed passwords, encrypted credential storage and rate limiting to protect your information.

Cookies

We use strictly necessary cookies for sign-in sessions and preferences. We do not use advertising trackers.

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and, for material changes, notify account holders by email.

Contact

Information officer and privacy queries: sales@nahalabs.co.za.
